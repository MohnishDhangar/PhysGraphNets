# Literature reading order and takeaways

1. Pfaff et al. (MeshGraphNets), https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03409 — encoder,
   processor, decoder and mesh representations.
2. Raissi et al. (PINNs), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2018.10.045 — strong-form
   constraints and limitations of per-instance solution fitting.
3. Gao et al. (physics-informed graph Galerkin),
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2021.114502 — weak-form constraints.
4. Würth et al. (PI-MeshGraphNets),
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2024.117102 — mesh-based unsupervised physics.
5. Li et al. (Multipole Graph Neural Operator),
   https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2020/hash/4b21cf96d4cf612f239a6c322b10c8fe-Abstract.html
   — nonlocal and multiscale interactions.
6. Kovachki et al. (Neural Operator), https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.08481 —
   operator learning and discretization independence.

For each paper record: PDE family, graph representation, physical residual,
training data requirements, mesh generalization, hardware, code availability,
reproducibility and limitations. Original PDFs can be placed in research/papers
where redistribution permissions permit; do not commit copyrighted PDFs.
